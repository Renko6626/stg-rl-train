The revised ECL samples one uniform initial BAM phase and advances the 20 fan centers by 18° (`main.ecl:26-43`). Each fan has 24° total width; the spawn phase centers therefore overlap by 6° before movement. The `±omega` is set at birth (`main.ecl:36-41`), so the warning itself rotates continuously, as do active and fade, consistent with the engine manual. There is no jump rotation or dependence on future RNG. Rank values and durations in TASK agree with the source; active is within the contract's 150–210 range. Full 640 px rays exceed the maximum 476 px source-to-playfield-corner distance. The endpoint-speed calculations in TASK are consistent: rank 3 is about 2.95 px/frame at 640 px and 2.19 px/frame at 476 px. The 4.5/2.0 speed, 4.5 px effective collision radius, and warning travel figures now match the feasibility configuration.

Both moving witnesses are rank 3 only: seed 1 survives 1793 frames (feasibility JSON record at line 3097; trajectory SHA256 `7bd87c315853cade7a8e166202eba62fa7a4c3a0e2e345fa1f33db67fba54f40`) and seed 7 survives 1747 frames (line 3596; SHA256 `14091ea0aedf6311fe91fb9bd7c8390d79bfc674f376f8f1aeace5ca84655e46`). Stationary coverage passed only for the tested target set; it does not prove that no broader safe region exists. Preserve this bounded conclusion in the final acceptance record.
